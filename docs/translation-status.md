`README-fa.md` published as a bilingual landing-page exception. Other Persian
source reports remain private. The phase reports are editorial translations and
concise scientific syntheses, not literal line-for-line editions.

Numerical values, uncertainty and negative findings are preserved. Where later
work superseded a claim, see [errata](../research/errata.md). Missing source
inputs are not replaced with new data. A release owner who requires full verbatim
translations should commission and review them before publication; this is a
declared documentation limitation, not a claim of complete translation.
