# Roadmap and status

- Completed: twenty bounded investigations, with negative and superseded results archived.
- Verified experimental capabilities: deterministic reconstruction, controlled recurrence discovery, exact reversible transforms, and shared-seed recovery on planted data.
- Phases 11–15: CGMC audit/controls, AMRD and cross-plane screens, scaling costs, 2-adic/coordinate/GF(2) discovery and image-function networks are complete. No general real-data mathematical gain was established.
- Phases 16–20: parameterized grammar, multidimensional algebra, exact spectral representations, sparse XOR dictionaries and structural attribution are complete for the tested settings. Exact executable-format dependencies replicated, but full-region description costs remained above conventional controls. The corrected Haar run supersedes the original standard-Haar claim; both are retained.
- Not established: general superiority, robust public decoder security, universal compression, or industrial throughput.

## Where to go next

The next research question is open, not a scheduled Phase 21 or a promise of improvement. Before building another codec, look for a source class with independently identifiable structure and estimate the complete cost of describing it. Distinguish the existence of mathematical relationships, their discovery, and the cost of applying them.

Possible directions for theoretical study include algorithmic statistics, minimum description length, representation theory, and the cost of alternative description languages. These are research questions, not established solutions. Prefer small, predeclared structural experiments with matched controls and a stop rule before committing to a new architecture.

Near term: independent replication of paired controls, review of publication rights and attribution, and portability checks on reviewed inputs.
