# Roadmap and status

- Completed: fifteen bounded investigations, with negative results archived.
- Verified capabilities: deterministic reconstruction, controlled recurrence
  discovery, exact transforms, and shared-seed recovery on planted data.
- Phase 11-15 status: CGMC audit/controls, AMRD/cross-plane screens, scaling costs,
  2-adic/coordinate/GF(2) discovery and image-function networks are complete.
  No general real-data mathematical gain was established.
- Not established: general superiority, robust public decoder security,
  universal compression, or industrial throughput.
- Near term: resolve publication rights and attribution; independently replicate
  the paired controls; expand portability only with reviewed tests.
- Research proposals are listed in [open questions](research/open-questions.md).
