# Phase 05 — SXZ4 independent spans and representation discovery

## 1. Research question
Can a model describe any independently located span without tying the
mathematical region to a storage-block boundary?

## 2. Motivation
Block-local detection and a single selected transform can hide useful regions
and force expensive fragmentation when the backend stream is split.

## 3. Hypothesis
A position map, independent spans, reversible representation transforms and
local residuals can preserve more structure than fixed blocks, provided every
map/descriptor cost is counted.

## 4. Experimental architecture
`spans4` separates probe positions, spans and storage streams. Models produce
descriptors and predictions; residuals are encoded byte-wise, word-wise or as
causal errors. A backend compresses the remaining stream.

## 5. Mathematical methods
Periodic/affine/quadratic/lane/LCG/xorshift/LFSR models; word-difference and
Rice coding; reversible transforms such as shuffle, delta, radix and float
views; local extension with tolerance and cost guards.

## 6. Dataset description
A 39-file panel (synthetic, real and precompressed groups) and an early public
suite. Some files are derived fixtures or slices; labels and sizes are in the
archives. No missing private fixture is represented by another file.

## 7. Baselines
Deflate-6, Zstd-19, XZ-6 and Brotli-11 at stated settings; SXZ3 and
transform-only/span-off variants. All decodes are checked.

## 8. Experimental protocol
Screen transforms, probe positions, fit models, extend spans, decide residuals,
serialize the map, pack the residual stream, decode the complete archive and
compare bytes. Backend and model gains are separated by ablation.

## 9. Evaluation metrics
Archive bytes, exactness, span coverage, model/residual/map bytes, probes and
time. Ratio alone is insufficient when inputs have different sizes.

## 10. Quantitative results
Initial benchmark rows are in [SXZ4 bench](../../lab/zip-protocol/results/sxz4_bench.json).
The phase showed measurable gains on planted and selected structured data and
little/no gain on many general files. The later validation supersedes initial
headline interpretations with a complete panel and attribution analysis.

## 11. Positive findings
Independent spans can represent laws crossing nominal blocks; reversible views
and exact residuals improve selected physiological and numeric data; guarding
by cost reduces fragmentation.

## 12. Negative findings
Span decisions based only on local cost can lose after global backend coding.
The transform screen can rank the best chain poorly on some data. Some model
families are effectively inactive until implementation defects are fixed.

## 13. Implementation limitations
Probe budget, fixed fit sample, scan granularity and descriptor overhead bound
discovery. Python performance is not production-grade.

## 14. Theoretical limitations
Position maps and descriptors are side information; selecting among many
spans increases search multiplicity without guaranteeing a net saving. The
method is conditional on local agreement and backend complementarity.

## 15. Conclusions
SXZ4 moved from block-bound to independently positioned descriptions and
made representation gains separable from formula gains. It did not show broad
real-data superiority; validation is authoritative.
