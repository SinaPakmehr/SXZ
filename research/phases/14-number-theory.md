# Phase 14 - Number Theory and Algebraic Discovery

## 1. Research question

Can compact number-theoretic or algebraic descriptions explain real binary data
beyond the linear recurrences, coordinate surfaces and fixed tiles already
tested? Three bounded episodes examine 2-adic rationals, coordinate functions
and quadratic Boolean relations.

## 2. 2-adic rational reconstruction

For a bit sequence `b[0..N-1]`, `A = sum(b[i]*2^i)`, `M = 2^N`. The lattice
generated by `(M,0)` and `(A,1)` is reduced with exact integer Gauss reduction;
small combinations in `[-2,2]^2` are tested. Candidates require odd `q>1`,
`p != 0`, normalization of sign/common factor and
`(p - A*q) mod M = 0`. Decoding is exact via
`b[i]=p mod 2; p=(p-b[i]*q)/2`.

Windows were 128/512 bits, non-overlapping, fit on the first half and required to
predict the second half exactly, then extended to the first mismatch (8192-bit
cap). Packed LSB-first bytes and eight bit planes were tested. Constant and
short-period structures were separated; a model is called nontrivial only when
its rational record is smaller than the best prefix+period description.

## 3. 2-adic data and results

Eight real files, three 5,461 B regions each (start/middle/end), 20,208 windows.
There were 98,698 bounded candidate checks and 63 exact accepted models (12
nontrivial, 51 simple). All 63 reconstruct exactly. Nontrivial coverage is 4,122
packed bits, all in the final 5,461 B of `7zr.exe`; no bit-plane nontrivial
coverage was found.

The 12 nontrivial models share `q=4,294,836,225=(2^16-1)^2`, with `p` of 27-32
bits. Spans are 138-538 bits; records are 18-20 B. Aggregate span-local costs:
raw 524 B, zstd-9 621 B, zstd-19 618 B, rational records 229 B, BM records
300 B. Eleven of 12 beat raw+zstd; all 12 beat the BM record. Example:
`p=-3,157,277,647`, `q=4,294,836,225`, span 538 bits, record 20 B, exact.

**Correction from the Episode 2 audit:** re-reading these records as
little-endian 16-bit words shows all 12 are exact arithmetic progressions with
at most one carry: `w[k]=(w0+k*d+carry*[k>=pos]) mod 2^16`. Ten use
`d=0x800` plus one `+1` carry; two have no carry. A compact AP record needs
14-16 B, not 18-20 B. The finite 16-bit periodic suffix does **not** imply an
infinite period of 16.

Archive-level estimate for the 7zr region: direct zstd-9 1,659 B, zstd-19
1,605 B; math-only 5,191 B; hybrid 2,211 B (zstd-9 literals) or 2,215 B
(zstd-19 literals). The hybrid is 552 B worse than direct zstd. Span-level
savings do not become archive savings.

## 4. Coordinate functions

Six real files, two non-overlapping 4 KiB regions each; W=16/64/256; u8/u16le;
degree-3 polynomials, additive `U(r)+V(c)`, XOR networks, and residual
compression. Development choices were frozen before validation. No exact
coordinate function was found. Residual entropy was 6.33-7.95 bits/byte; `C`
was 40-1,472 B worse than direct zstd-9 on every file. The best approximate PDF
polynomial had ten coefficients but no exact coverage.

## 5. Quadratic Boolean relations

Causal contexts 8/16 (37/137 quadratic features; 9/17 linear controls), 1,024-bit
windows, 512-bit fit and 512-bit validation, packed bits and bit planes. Exact
bit-packed GF(2) elimination recorded rank/nullity and inconsistent systems.
Development: 70/71 inconsistent, one train-exact model, zero validation-exact.
Validation: 69/71 inconsistent, two train-exact, zero validation-exact. The one
development model was underdetermined (nullity 701) and reached 339/512 bits on
its own held-out half. Degree-1 controls were no better.

Implicit relations used 46/154 degree-2 monomials including `y`; the
homogeneous nullspace was filtered for input-only and non-unique candidates.
**0 predictive relations** were found on development or validation for either
context. Nullity was often large (e.g. 803 for weights L16 development), but
that is not evidence without unique validation. No Gröbner basis was computed
because no predictive relation set survived; no dependency was installed.

**Correction/qualification:** per-window nullity is not interpreted as a
standalone law, and aggregate rank accounting is reported only as sums. The ECG
960/1,024 bit accuracy for two validation-consistent GF(2) models is **bit
accuracy before residual compression**, not an actual compressed-residual size.

## 6. Positive findings

A real span-level 2-adic description exists in the 7zr padding region; it is
slightly smaller than BM on those spans and reconstructs exactly. Gaussian
elimination, lattice reduction and serialization are exact; all accepted
records reconstruct; the AP+carry simplification is real.

## 7. Negative findings and limitations

No archive-level improvement; no new class missed by BM; coordinate functions
failed; quadratic/implicit relations produced no validation-exact predictor;
degree-1 controls matched the nonlinear result; no Gröbner basis was claimed.
Inputs are sampled regions, not full files. The 2-adic positive is one edge
region and should not be generalized.
