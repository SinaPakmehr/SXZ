# Phase 01 — Original mathematical hypothesis and SXZ1

## 1. Research question
Can a finite deterministic formula plus parameters exactly replace a chosen
part of a byte stream more cheaply than storing that part?

## 2. Motivation
A lossless codec can store a generator description instead of repeating bytes.
The scientific question is whether real files contain economically useful
instances, not whether formulas can generate some data.

## 3. Hypothesis
Short periodic, affine, polynomial, recurrence, constant or bit-generator
models may describe selected windows. The description, parameters, location
and residual must all cost less than the literal alternative.

## 4. Experimental architecture
SXZ1 (`sinuxz`) scans bounded windows, fits a model bank, extends a selected
model, and falls back to literal, PCM/image probes or Deflate. A position map
and controlled descriptors reconstruct exact bytes.

## 5. Mathematical methods
Affine and quadratic recurrences modulo word widths, periodicity, reference
constant slices, and finite linear or bit-state generators. Model selection
is a byte-level cost comparison rather than a universal shortest-program search.

## 6. Dataset description
Small generated laws are capability controls. Text, binaries, images and
reference-slice probes are practical negatives or exploratory inputs from the
historical workspace. Some early inputs cannot be redistributed; see
[data provenance](../../datasets/README.md).

## 7. Baselines
Raw storage and Deflate at documented settings; later phases add Zstd, XZ and
Brotli. An absent formula is not compared as if it were a compressed stream.

## 8. Experimental protocol
Fit on a sample, extend while agreement and residual cost permit, serialize a
descriptor and position map, decode, and compare every byte. Measure final
archive bytes, not only matched coverage.

## 9. Evaluation metrics
Exact round trip, archive bytes, formula coverage and span count. Encode and
decode time are secondary and settings-specific.

## 10. Quantitative results
Historical exploratory tables are preserved in the
[model benchmark](../../lab/zip-protocol/results/model_benchmark.md). The
early milestone was correct reconstruction of controlled laws; no
general-purpose compression win was established.

## 11. Positive findings
Finite exact models and reversible residuals can be encoded and replayed.
Fallback keeps the format lossless when no model is selected.

## 12. Negative findings
Short-window scanning cannot establish absence of structure. Formula hits can
cost more than the bytes they replace, especially when backend context is
split. A match is not an archive saving.

## 13. Implementation limitations
Search is budgeted, widths and model families are incomplete, and early
fallback choices include Deflate and simple media probes rather than a full
modern codec matrix.

## 14. Theoretical limitations
Kolmogorov complexity is uncomputable; searching a finite bank cannot identify
an arbitrary shortest description. For a fixed generative family the number of
short descriptions is limited by counting, irrespective of search speed.

## 15. Conclusions
SXZ1 established engineering exactness, not general superiority. Its central
claim is conditional: a file is compressible by this method only when a checked
description beats the literal and backend alternatives after full accounting.

## 16. Reproduction instructions
Install the package and run the offline planted demo as described in the
[reproduction guide](../../docs/reproduction.md). The historical model table is
preserved rather than regenerated to complete missing inputs.
