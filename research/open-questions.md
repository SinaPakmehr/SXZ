# Open questions

Can a finite generative dictionary beat a paired conventional backend on a
source class with independent provenance? Can exact reversible representations
expose additional structure after controlling for existing specialist codecs?
Can related checkpoints share descriptions after paying for the reference,
alignment, location map and residual? Can address structure reduce conditional
cost without hiding inputs in the decoder? When does computational asymmetry
remain useful under decoder resource limits? Could a probabilistic generative
model beat a direct code under the same distribution and side information?

After Phases 16–20, further questions concern the distinction between discovering
structure and economically describing it. Can algorithmic statistics help
separate a useful model from its unexplained data? Can a different algebraic
representation reduce the total cost, including the representation itself?
Can knowledge of the generating process yield independent gains after comparison
with format-aware codecs? Can a structural census and a lower-cost estimate
rule out unpromising model families before implementation?

These are falsifiable questions or directions for theoretical study, not
discoveries or a commitment to Phase 21. Prefer a small experiment with an
independently chosen holdout and a predeclared stop rule.
