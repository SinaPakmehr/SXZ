# Limitations

Historical panels vary in size, diversity, codec settings and use of complete
files versus slices. Synthetic laws identify capability, not prevalence.
Some archival data and tools cannot be publicly reconstructed. Candidate
caps, sampling, state budgets and heuristics prevent claims of optimality.
Exactness tests do not establish secure handling of arbitrary input.
Windows-only historical timing paths may require adaptations on other systems.
See [reproduction status](../docs/reproduction.md).
